## Section A (2 Marks Each)

### 1. Define “Conscious Full Spectrum Response” Model.

The Conscious Full Spectrum Response (CFSR) Model is an approach to decision-making that encourages individuals to respond thoughtfully and ethically by considering emotional, intellectual, social, and moral aspects of a situation rather than reacting impulsively. 

### 2. Differentiate between Judgement and Discernment.

**Judgement** is the process of forming an opinion or conclusion, often based on limited information or personal beliefs. **Discernment** is the ability to carefully evaluate situations using wisdom, understanding, and objectivity to arrive at fair and ethical decisions. 

### 3. What is meant by an Integrated Personality?

An Integrated Personality is a balanced personality in which thoughts, emotions, values, and actions are aligned. Such individuals demonstrate consistency, self-awareness, ethical behavior, and emotional stability in their personal and professional lives. 

### 4. Define Assertiveness and explain its importance in Professional Ethics.

Assertiveness is the ability to express one's opinions, needs, and concerns confidently and respectfully without violating the rights of others. In professional ethics, assertiveness helps individuals communicate honestly, resolve conflicts effectively, and uphold ethical standards. 

### 5. What is meant by Professional Ethics?

Professional Ethics refers to the moral principles, values, and standards of conduct that guide the behavior of individuals in their professional roles. It promotes honesty, integrity, accountability, fairness, and responsibility.

### 6. Explain the concept of MULYA PRAVAH 2.0 and its significance in developing ethical awareness among youth.

MULYA PRAVAH 2.0 is a value-based educational initiative aimed at nurturing ethical awareness, character development, and responsible citizenship among young people. The program focuses on helping students understand human values, self-awareness, empathy, ethical reasoning, and social responsibility.

Its significance lies in encouraging youth to reflect on their beliefs, attitudes, and actions. It promotes values such as honesty, respect, compassion, responsibility, and integrity. Through discussions, self-enquiry, experiential learning, and community engagement, students develop the ability to make ethical decisions in personal and professional life.

MULYA PRAVAH 2.0 also helps young people manage conflicts, appreciate diversity, and contribute positively to society. By strengthening moral character and ethical understanding, it prepares students to become responsible citizens and ethical professionals. 

### 7. Discuss how contentious arguments can be transformed into meaningful discussions through empathy and active listening. Give suitable examples.

Contentious arguments often arise because individuals focus on defending their viewpoints rather than understanding others. Empathy and active listening play a crucial role in transforming such conflicts into meaningful discussions.

Empathy involves understanding and appreciating another person's feelings and perspectives. Active listening requires paying full attention, avoiding interruptions, and responding thoughtfully. When people feel heard and respected, they become more open to dialogue and cooperation.

For example, during a disagreement among students regarding project responsibilities, active listening allows each member to express concerns without fear of criticism. Empathy helps others understand the reasons behind those concerns, leading to constructive solutions.

Similarly, workplace conflicts between employees and management can be resolved through respectful communication and mutual understanding. By focusing on shared goals rather than personal differences, contentious arguments can become opportunities for learning, collaboration, and positive change. 

### 8. Describe the role of Human Values and Professional Ethics in Higher Education Institutions. How do they contribute to personality development and social harmony?

Human Values and Professional Ethics are essential components of higher education because they help students develop both competence and character. Educational institutions play a vital role in nurturing values such as honesty, respect, responsibility, empathy, and social commitment.

These values contribute to personality development by promoting self-awareness, emotional intelligence, leadership skills, and ethical decision-making. Students learn to respect diversity, cooperate with others, and act responsibly in academic and professional environments.

Professional ethics guide future professionals in maintaining integrity, accountability, and fairness in their careers. Ethical education also helps reduce misconduct, corruption, and unethical practices.

Furthermore, human values promote mutual respect, tolerance, and peaceful coexistence, thereby strengthening social harmony. By combining knowledge with ethical awareness, higher education institutions prepare students to become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

# Section C (11 Marks Each)

### 9. Discuss the concept of “Values and Skills for Youth” in detail. Explain how ethical values, assertiveness, and integrated personality help students become responsible citizens and professionals.

The concept of “Values and Skills for Youth” focuses on developing both moral character and practical competencies required for success in life. Modern society demands not only technical knowledge but also ethical awareness, emotional intelligence, communication skills, and social responsibility.

Ethical values such as honesty, integrity, compassion, respect, and responsibility provide a moral foundation for decision-making. These values guide students in distinguishing right from wrong and encourage ethical conduct in personal and professional life.

Assertiveness is an important skill that enables individuals to communicate confidently and respectfully. Assertive students can express their opinions, defend ethical principles, and resolve conflicts without aggression or passivity. This improves leadership abilities and interpersonal relationships.

An integrated personality refers to harmony among thoughts, emotions, values, and actions. Individuals with integrated personalities demonstrate self-confidence, emotional stability, self-discipline, and consistency in behavior. They are better equipped to face challenges and make balanced decisions.

Together, ethical values, assertiveness, and integrated personality contribute to the development of responsible citizens and professionals. They help students become trustworthy leaders, effective communicators, and socially responsible individuals who contribute positively to their communities and workplaces. 

### 10. Explain the strategies used to transform contentious arguments into constructive dialogue. Discuss the role of communication, empathy, and ethical thinking in creating a space for listening and positive change.

Constructive dialogue is essential for resolving conflicts and building healthy relationships. Transforming contentious arguments into meaningful discussions requires specific strategies and a commitment to respectful communication.

One important strategy is active listening, which involves giving full attention to the speaker and seeking to understand their perspective. Another strategy is maintaining respect even during disagreements. Avoiding personal attacks and focusing on issues rather than individuals helps reduce hostility.

Communication plays a central role in constructive dialogue. Clear, honest, and respectful communication helps participants express their views effectively while minimizing misunderstandings. Empathy enables individuals to understand the emotions and concerns of others, creating trust and mutual respect.

Ethical thinking encourages fairness, open-mindedness, and responsibility. It helps individuals evaluate situations objectively and consider the impact of their actions on others. Ethical reasoning promotes solutions that benefit all parties rather than serving narrow interests.

When communication, empathy, and ethical thinking work together, they create an environment where people feel heard and valued. Such an environment promotes cooperation, conflict resolution, and positive social change. Therefore, these qualities are essential for transforming arguments into productive dialogue. 

### 11. Explain the importance of Professional Ethics in Higher Education Institutions. Discuss various strategies for inculcating ethical values and well-being among students and faculty members.

Professional Ethics is essential in Higher Education Institutions because it promotes integrity, accountability, fairness, and respect among students, teachers, and administrators. Ethical institutions create an environment that supports learning, trust, and academic excellence.

Professional ethics helps prevent misconduct such as plagiarism, cheating, discrimination, and corruption. It encourages honesty in research, transparency in administration, and responsible behavior in professional practice. Ethical conduct strengthens the credibility and reputation of educational institutions.

Several strategies can be used to inculcate ethical values and well-being among students and faculty members. These include integrating ethics courses into the curriculum, organizing workshops and seminars on values, promoting community service activities, and encouraging reflective learning practices.

Institutions can also provide counseling services, mindfulness programs, and wellness initiatives to support mental and emotional well-being. Faculty members should act as role models by demonstrating ethical behavior in teaching, research, and professional interactions.

By promoting ethical values and holistic well-being, higher education institutions contribute to the development of responsible professionals, ethical leaders, and socially conscious citizens. Thus, professional ethics is a cornerstone of quality education and sustainable societal development.
